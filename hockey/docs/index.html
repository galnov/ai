How it learned

The blue player is a small neural network (12 inputs, two layers of 128 neurons, 2 outputs). It sees what you see: both paddles and the puck, their positions and speeds. It answers with the direction to move its paddle, 30 times a second.

Nobody told it how to play. It started with random moves, against a simple rule-based bot. Then it played more and more against earlier versions of itself. It gets a reward for scoring and a penalty for conceding.

To keep it fair, it reacts 67 ms late (like a quick human), and its paddle has exactly your top speed and acceleration.
