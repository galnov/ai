How the driver learned

Each car drives with the "brain" from a different point in training. At first the network knows nothing, and within a few hundred updates it is already driving.

Brain of AI

The network reads from left to right: 9 sensors and the speed, two hidden layers (14 of the 128 neurons in each layer are shown), and a Q value for each action. The chosen action is highlighted in yellow. Green lines are positive weights, red ones are negative.
