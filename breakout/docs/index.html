Pixel Breakout AI

A convolutional network that learned Breakout from nothing but the screen's pixels and the score, like DeepMind's Atari player.

Move your paddle with the mouse, your finger or the arrow keys. Same bricks, same serves: whoever breaks more bricks with 3 balls wins.

How it learned

The network gets two gray screens of 48 × 48 pixels, the one before and the one now, and chooses: left, stay or right. Nobody told it what a ball or a paddle is. It only gets +1 for a brick and −1 for a lost ball.

Two convolutional layers learn to find the shapes on the screen, and comparing two screens tells it which way the ball moves. It learned with PPO, playing 64 games at a time for millions of moves.
